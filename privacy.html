Privacy Policy

Ledger (记账) · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Ledger is a personal expense tracker that runs in your browser. It has no server of its own. This page explains what happens to your data.

Where your data is stored

  • Your records, categories, budget and settings are stored in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive.
  • A copy of your recent data is also kept on your own device (browser storage) so the app opens quickly and works offline.
  • The developer of Ledger does not run any server or database, and cannot see your data.

Google account access

  • When you sign in with Google, Ledger asks for permission to use only the files it creates or that you choose to open with it (the drive.file permission). It cannot see or change any other file in your Google Drive.
  • Ledger also reads your email address to show which account you are signed in with.
  • Your data goes directly between your device and Google. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Ledger’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of data.
  • Your data is never used for any purpose other than showing it back to you in the app.

Removing your data

  • Delete the Ledger Sheet from your Google Drive to remove your records.
  • Remove Ledger’s access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clear the site data in your browser (or delete the app from your home screen) to remove the copy on your device.

隐私政策

记账（Ledger）· 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 20 日

记账是在浏览器里运行的个人记账 App，没有自己的服务器。以下说明你的资料会怎样处理。

资料存在哪里

  • 你的记录、分类、预算和设置，存在你自己 Google Drive 里的 Google Sheet。
  • 为了打开更快、没有网络也能用，最近的资料也会存一份在你自己的手机（浏览器）里。
  • 记账的开发者没有任何服务器或数据库，看不到你的资料。

Google 帐号权限

  • 用 Google 登录时，记账只会要求使用它自己建立、或你选择用它打开的文件（drive.file 权限），看不到也改不了你 Google Drive 里的其他文件。
  • 记账也会读取你的电邮地址，用来显示你登录的是哪个帐号。
  • 资料直接在你的手机和 Google 之间传送，不会传给开发者或任何其他人。
  • 记账对 Google API 资料的使用，遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 规定。

我们不会做的事

  • 没有广告，没有分析或追踪，不会出售或分享资料。
  • 你的资料只会用来在 App 里显示给你自己看。

删除资料

  • 在 Google Drive 删除记账的 Sheet，就会删除你的记录。
  • 随时可以在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 取消记账的权限。
  • 清除浏览器的网站资料（或删除主屏幕上的 App），就会删除手机里的那一份。
